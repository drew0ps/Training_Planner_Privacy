TrainingPlanner

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

The short version

TrainingPlanner doesn't collect your data. Your training stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app contains no third-party code that collects data.

What the app stores

The app stores your programs, workout logs, program summaries, body entries (body weight, body fat and measurements), progress photos and settings, and, if you're a trainer, the reports your clients send you and the programs you prepare for them. They are kept on your device and, when you're signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud database, so they stay in sync on your devices. Only you can see your private iCloud data: the developer has no access to it. Apple stores it under the Apple Media Services and iCloud terms and Apple's privacy policy.

You can delete your data at any time by deleting programs in the app, by deleting the app, or under Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › TrainingPlanner.

Apple Health

Only if you turn it on, the app saves each finished workout to Apple Health as strength training: its start and end time, the session name, the number of sets, the total reps, the training volume and the average effort (RPE). It also saves the body weight, body fat percentage and waist measurement of the body entries you log; progress photos and other measurements are never saved to Apple Health. The app doesn't read any data from Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone; it isn't sent to the developer. You can turn this off in the app's Settings or in the Health app.

Apple Intelligence

Designing programs and coaching use Apple Intelligence on your device. Your training data is processed on the device and isn't sent to the developer or to third parties.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. The developer doesn't receive your payment details or Apple Account information; the app only checks with the App Store on your device whether you have TrainingPlanner Pro.

Working with a trainer

Only when you choose to, the app makes a report file of your name, programs, workout logs for the period you pick, and program summaries, for you to send to your trainer with AirDrop, Messages, Mail or another app. A trainer sends programs back the same way. These files go only to the people you send them to, through the app you choose, which handles them under its own privacy policy; nothing goes through the developer. A trainer keeps their clients' reports on their device and in their private iCloud, and a client's changes from a trainer are only taken when the client accepts them.

If you're a trainer, your clients' reports contain their training data: keep them only as long as you work together, and remove a client in the app when you stop.

Children

TrainingPlanner isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page, with a new effective date.
